Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Google OAuth client lets rclone, running on its owner's computer, access the owner's own Google Drive. It is used only by its owner.

Data accessed

Files and file metadata in the owner's Google Drive, only when the owner runs rclone.

How data is used and stored

Data moves directly between Google's servers and the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to, collected by or stored by the developer or any third party. There are no servers, analytics, cookies or advertising.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, shared or transferred. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
